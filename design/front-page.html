1Shenzhen · APEC summit

Trump, Xi and Putin sign a “declaration towards peace”

Ninety minutes, four points: UN reform, more refined oil, limits on the ICC and talks on nuclear risk. No arms-control deal, and no mention of Ukraine or Iran.

2The Hague · justice

World Court: Myanmar committed genocide

By 9 votes to 6, the ICJ holds a state to account under the Genocide Convention for the first time, over the 2017 Rohingya campaign.

3Antalya · climate

COP31 draft drops fossil fuels and $120bn

The revised cover text removes both references to coal, oil and gas, and the adaptation-finance target, a day before the summit closes.

4Vice City · games
